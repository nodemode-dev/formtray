# Demo credits: Widgets and layout

## Window screenshots

Each is the verified demo screenshot of an Apache-2.0 sibling Form app, taken from that app's own demo, resized to at most 1280 px wide and re-encoded as WebP (sources `../../demo/src/shots/`). They appear as windows on the desktop, as the Windows view plates and as large Widgets tiles. The window interface in each is that app's own work (Apache-2.0). Every photograph, clip or recording visible in a capture is credited in that app's own `demo/CREDITS.md` and listed below. Screenshots of the GPL-3.0 apps (FormPortal, FormSkills, FormMCP) are not used; they show their icon only.
